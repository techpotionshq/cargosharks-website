# Questions, and when something looks wrong

#### My WhatsApp reply was refused.

WhatsApp only accepts a free reply within 24 hours of the customer's last message. The conversation panel warns you before you send. Ask the customer to write again, or contact them another way.

#### Price it says it needs a house margin.

Only a buying rate covers this lane and the customer has no house margin for this branch. Open the customer, choose the branch under [{app:customers.termsBranch}]{.ui}, set *House margin* and *Margin*, save, and press [{app:quote.priceIt}]{.ui} again. Or load a selling rate card for the lane.

#### Price it says there is no rate for this lane.

No live rate card covers the route, mode and date. Use [{app:rateRequests.request}]{.ui}, or ask your operations manager to load a card.

#### Creating a quotation says the price is below cost.

The selling price is lower than the buying price. Check the rate card, the customer's margin and the currency before sending.

#### The customer says the quotation has expired.

A quotation expires by itself after its validity date and can no longer be accepted. Revise it to send a new version with a new date.

#### The assistant did not answer the customer.

Check the Intake agent panel: it records every turn and the reason it stayed quiet, such as an automatic reply, a colleague having taken over, or no model configured. Check also that the agent is on for the branch under Settings.

#### A form says someone else changed the record.

Another person saved the same record while you had the form open. The form has already refreshed itself with their values; make your change again and save.

#### A booking is refused because of credit.

The customer is on hold or past their credit limit. An operations manager or finance can record an override on the customer's page, with a reason.

#### Sign-in says to wait.

After several wrong passwords or two-factor codes in a row, sign-in pauses for a few minutes. Wait, then try again carefully, or use the forgotten password link.

#### I cannot see a record I expected.

It may belong to a branch you hold no role in. You see only your branches, and a record elsewhere shows as not found.

#### A screen says I cannot view it.

Your role does not include that permission. The message names it; send that name to your administrator.

#### A download failed.

The error page says so in both languages and offers to try again. If it fails twice, send the page's address to your administrator.

#### A number looks wrong.

Do not overwrite it. Open the record's history; the audit log shows what changed and when.

#### Can I delete something?

No. Close, cancel, supersede or deactivate it. That is what makes the records usable as evidence later.
