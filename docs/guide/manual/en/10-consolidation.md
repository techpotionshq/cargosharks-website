# Consolidation

::: intro
Putting several customers' smaller shipments in one container, under one master bill of lading.
:::

## What it is for

A consolidation is a master shipment with several house shipments attached. The shipping line issues one **master bill** to you; you issue a **house bill** to each customer. The master cost is then split across the houses.

![**Consolidations.** Each master, its carrier, route, houses and volume.](shot:/consolidations)

## Step by step

::: steps
1.  [New consolidation]{.ui}: mode, ports, carrier, master bill, booking, vessel and dates.
2.  [Attach]{.ui} the shipments sailing under it. [Detach]{.ui} one that moves to another sailing.
3.  [Add cost]{.ui} for the master freight, then [Split]{.ui} it by volume, weight, packages, equally or by hand.
4.  [Close manifest]{.ui} at the cut-off, and print the [Manifest]{.ui} PDF.
:::

## Good to know

-   **Could share a container**, at the foot of the screen, groups loose shipments on the same lane and sailing and shows how full a container they would make.
-   **Split** belongs to operations managers and finance, because it decides every house's margin. [Reverse]{.ui} posts the opposite rather than deleting.
