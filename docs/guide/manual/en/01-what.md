# What the system is

::: intro
One page to understand the business, the problem and the software.
:::

## What a freight forwarder does

When a company in Kuwait buys steel coils from a mill in China, somebody has to arrange how they get there. That somebody is a **freight forwarder**. A forwarder does not usually own ships, planes or trucks. It buys space from the companies that do, such as shipping lines, airlines and trucking firms, and it sells the whole journey to its customer as one service.

A forwarder's day is made of a few repeating jobs: answering requests for a price, asking partners what they will charge, sending quotations, booking the space, following the cargo, preparing the paperwork, helping it through customs, and finally sending the invoice and collecting the money.

## The problem

Most forwarders still run this work across WhatsApp chats, inboxes, spreadsheets and an accounting package that knows nothing about the shipment. Prices get lost in threads. Nobody is sure who is answering which customer. A container sits at the port for an extra week and the daily charges are only noticed when the bill arrives. The invoice goes out late, or with the wrong figures.

## What the system does

{brand} keeps every step in one place, on one record per shipment, from the customer's first message to the money in the bank. The system reads incoming WhatsApp messages and emails, asks the customer for missing details, collects prices from partners, works out the selling price, produces the quotation, the shipping documents and the invoice, and keeps a clock on everything that can cost money if it is forgotten.

::: tip
**The human always stays in control**

The assistant can read, suggest and draft. It never sends a quotation, issues an invoice or changes a record on its own decision. A person accepts each suggestion, presses send, and signs off the money.
:::

## Who uses it

| Who | What they do in the system |
|---|---|
| Sales | Answer enquiries, send quotations, record what was won or lost. |
| Coordinators | Book and follow shipments, add containers, record milestones, issue documents. |
| Operations managers | Everything a coordinator does, plus rate cards, partners, reference data and reversals. |
| Finance | Issue invoices, record receipts, approve and pay supplier bills, settle agent accounts. |
| Customs brokers | Follow customs declarations and the dates clearance depends on. |
| Administrators | Users, branches, mailboxes, WhatsApp numbers, integrations and the brand. |
| Customers | Track shipments, accept quotations and see invoices in the customer portal. |
| Agents | Overseas partners see the shipments they are named on in the agent portal. |
