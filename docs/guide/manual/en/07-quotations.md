# Quotations

::: intro
The offer you send the customer: the price, what is included, and how long it is valid.
:::

## What it is for

Pricing an enquiry creates a draft quotation. The quotation page shows the price quoted, what it costs you, the margin, and the validity date, with the charge lines and a covering note.

![**A quotation.** Price, cost, margin and validity, then the lines and the note.](shot:/quotes/quote-316)

## Step by step

::: steps
1.  Open the quotation from [Quotations]{.ui}.
2.  Check the lines. [Add a line]{.ui} adds a charge if needed.
3.  Write a covering note, or press [Draft with the model]{.ui} to have one drafted in English or Arabic, then [Save note]{.ui}. The model writes words only, never figures.
4.  Open [PDF (EN)]{.ui} or [PDF (AR)]{.ui} to see exactly what the customer receives. Until it is sent, the PDF is marked as a draft.
5.  Press [{app:quoteDetail.moveOpen.sent}]{.ui} and confirm with [Send it]{.ui}.
6.  When the customer answers, record it with [Mark accepted]{.ui}, or let them accept or decline in the portal.
:::

![**The quotation PDF.** Your company name, the route, the charges, the covering note and what is included. The branch's trading conditions print at the foot once they are set under Settings, Branches.](shot:/api/quotes/quote-316/pdf?pdf=1)

## Good to know

-   **The margin freezes when you send.** A later rate change does not alter a sent quotation.
-   **Revise, never edit.** A revision is a new version and the old one is kept, marked superseded.
-   **Quotations expire by themselves** after their validity date, and the customer can no longer accept them.
-   **A price below cost is refused** with a message, so a mistake cannot reach the customer unnoticed.
