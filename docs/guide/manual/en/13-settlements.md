# Settlements

::: intro
Netting what an agent owes you against what you owe them, so one payment moves instead of two.
:::

## What it is for

An overseas agent is often both a supplier and a customer. A settlement takes a period, lists every open document on both sides, and shows *They owe us*, *We owe them*, the amount set off and the residual.

![**Settlements.** Each agent's netted account for a period.](shot:/settlements)

## Step by step

::: steps
1.  On the partner's page, link them under *Current account* to the customer record they are invoiced as.
2.  [Draft settlement]{.ui}: branch, agent, period, and the agreed rate if two currencies are involved.
3.  [Agree statement]{.ui} when both sides sign it off, then [Residual settled]{.ui} when the balancing payment moves.
:::

## Good to know

-   Running a settlement belongs to finance and operations managers. A voided settlement stays on the list with its reason.
