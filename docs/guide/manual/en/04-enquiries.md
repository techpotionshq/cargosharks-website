# Enquiries

::: intro
An enquiry is a customer asking for a price. Everything commercial starts here.
:::

## What it is for

The enquiry queue lists every request across the branches you work in, whether it came by WhatsApp, email, the portal, phone or a walk-in. Each one has an owner and a reply deadline, counted in working hours.

![**The enquiry queue.** Channel, customer, route, owner, status and the clock each enquiry is answering against.](shot:/enquiries)

## Step by step

::: steps
1.  **Open the enquiry.** New ones from WhatsApp or email are already in the queue. For a phone call, press [New enquiry]{.ui} and fill in what you know; only the branch and a way to reach the customer are required.
2.  **Read the conversation.** The **Conversation** panel shows every message with the customer, on WhatsApp or email, and what the assistant sent.
3.  **Check the suggestions.** Under **Read from the message**, each detail the assistant found is shown with the words it came from and how sure it is. Press [Accept]{.ui} or [Dismiss]{.ui} on each, or [{app:proposals.acceptAll}]{.ui}.
4.  **Fill in the rest with Edit details.** Under [More]{.ui}, [Edit details]{.ui} lets you correct the customer, mode, route, goods, Incoterm, dates, weights and containers.
5.  **Assign an owner.** [More]{.ui} then [Assign]{.ui}. An enquiry without an owner has nobody's name on the clock.
6.  **Reply from the app.** Type in **Reply to the customer** and press [Send reply]{.ui}. It goes out on the channel the customer used, in your name.
7.  **Get a price.** Request rates from partners, or press [{app:quote.priceIt}]{.ui} if a rate card already covers the route. See the chapter on rates.
8.  **Close it.** When the customer decides, [Close]{.ui} records won or lost, with a reason for a loss. [Cancel it]{.ui} is for an enquiry that will never be quoted.
:::

## Good to know

-   **The clock stops** when a quotation is sent, or when you press [Mark replied]{.ui} to record a reply you made outside the system. Opening the enquiry does not stop it.
-   **A person's typing always wins.** A suggestion for a field someone has already filled is marked *Already filled* and cannot overwrite it.
-   **Mistakes are explained where they are.** If a field is wrong, the message appears under that field, in the language you are working in. If someone else changed the enquiry while you had Edit details open, the form refreshes itself with the new values and asks you to make your change again, so nothing is overwritten by accident.
-   **WhatsApp has a 24 hour rule.** A free reply is only accepted within 24 hours of the customer's last message. After that, the panel warns you and WhatsApp refuses the send until the customer writes again.
-   **Lost is not cancelled.** Lost means you competed and did not win. Cancelled means there was nothing to win. Keeping them apart keeps your win rate honest.
