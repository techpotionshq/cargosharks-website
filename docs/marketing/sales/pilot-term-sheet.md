## 1. What this is

A **three-month paid pilot**, on your real jobs, on your real lanes, with your
branding, in your own deployment. Not a demonstration environment and not a
trial licence — the same software, the same database, the same documents you
would run in production.

At the end of it you will know one thing that no demonstration can tell you:
whether a job goes from enquiry to invoice without leaving the system, and
whether your finance team can close a month off it.

## 3. What is included

- Deployment in the region you choose, provisioned and operated by us
- Two entities, per-branch users, roles and permissions
- Migration of your customers, contacts, agents, hauliers, ports and places
- Your branding applied end to end — screens, portal, documents, letterhead
- One rate card imported; milestone templates matched to your vocabulary
- Document templates: invoice, quotation, delivery order, packing list, manifest
- Public tracking page and customer portal, both bilingual
- Training: two sessions per role
- Support throughout, from a named person, not a ticket queue
- **Three change-request days per month** — see §5

- **Direct customs filing.** Declaration references, dates, duty and SLA are
  tracked. The broker still files.

---

## 5. Change requests

Bug fixes and configuration changes are not change requests and do not consume
days — those are simply support.

A change request is new work: a screen, a document, a field, a report. You get
**three days per month**, rolling, up to a maximum of six carried at any time.
Anything beyond that is quoted before it starts.

This clause exists to protect both sides. Without a number, "continuous
improvement" becomes an argument in month three.

---

## 6. What we ask in return

The rate in §2 is granted for these, and they form part of the agreement:

1. **Named reference.** We may name you as a customer, publish a short case
   study, and put you in front of prospects for a phone call — no more than
   `[four]` per year, at your convenience.
2. **Message corpus.** Permission to use your enquiry emails and WhatsApp
   messages, **de-identified**, to build and evaluate automatic intake. This is
   the feature you most want and it cannot be built without real messages. Your
   commercial data is never used for any other purpose and never leaves your
   deployment for any other reason.
3. **Two introductions** to forwarders in your network.
4. **A named owner on your side** who can make decisions in one meeting.

## 7. What "it worked" means

Agreed now, in writing, so that neither side argues about it in month three:

| # | Test |
|---|---|
| 1 | A job runs enquiry → quotation → award → execution → documents → invoice without leaving the system |
| 2 | Finance closes one calendar month from the system's figures |
| 3 | Your customers can see their own shipments without telephoning your office |
| 4 | Both languages work on every screen your staff actually use |

Month one goes to migration, branding and training. You are really judging the
product on months two and three — that is normal, and it is worth expecting.
