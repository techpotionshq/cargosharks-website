Fetched straight from the Unsplash CDN as WebP rather than downloaded and
re-encoded, which is what the four above needed:

  https://images.unsplash.com/<photo-id>?w=760&q=62&fm=webp&fit=crop

760px because the panel renders them at about 330 CSS pixels, so that is 2x for
a retina screen and there is nothing left for sharp to do. They were fetched at
w=1000 first, which put one of them at 340 KB for a thumbnail nobody sees until
they open a menu; 760 halves the set to 436 KB total.

**They are files in this directory, not hot-linked.** Nothing under `src/`
references images.unsplash.com — the CDN is where they came from, once, and the
build copies these three into `out/pic/photos/` like every other asset. A
marketing site that fetched its own furniture from a third party at run time
would also be handing that third party a log of every visitor, which is exactly
what `/privacy/` says does not happen.

A fourth was downloaded and then deleted rather than used: a warehouse
interior with a forklift (Bernd Dittrich, unsplash.com/photos/F2C_mSrb6iM).
It is a good photograph and it would have been a false promise — the product
has no warehouse module, and `/what-we-do/` §07 says so in as many words. The
"Trust" panel uses containers-aerial.webp instead, which had been sitting
unused since August.
