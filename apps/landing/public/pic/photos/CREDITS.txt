Downloaded 24 August 2026 at w=2000, q=72, and re-encoded to WebP on 25 August.
Only the WebP is kept. The JPEGs were the download rather than a source anyone
edits, and the four of them were 2.8 MB in a directory that now serves 1 MB.

To redo it: re-download from the URLs above and re-encode with sharp, the way
docs/marketing/capture.mjs does for the screenshots — that file carries the
import path and the reason sharp is reached by path rather than declared.

  sharp(input).resize({ width: W }).webp({ quality: Q }).toFile(output)

W and Q differ per file, because only one of these is looked at:

  hero-port-dusk       2000  76   full-bleed behind the headline
  containers-aerial    1600  62   a divider strip, cropped to a band
  containers-stack     1600  58   sits under two blur layers
  ship-aerial          1600  62   sits under the pilot section's dark scrim
